📋 Privacy Policy

My Roster — by Smart Nurse

Last updated: July 2026

Summary: My Roster stores all your data locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

1. Introduction

My Roster ("the App") is developed by Smart Nurse. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application.

2. Data Collection

My Roster does NOT collect any personal data. All data entered in the app — including roster information, salary data, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. We do not transmit any data to external servers.

3. Data Storage

All roster data, salary information, and app settings are stored locally on your device using secure local storage. No data leaves your device under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Services

My Roster does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Camera and Photo Library Access

The app may request access to your camera or photo library solely for the purpose of importing roster images for OCR text scanning. These images are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

6. File Access

The app requests file access solely to allow you to import roster files (Excel, PDF, Word, Image, CSV) from your device. These files are processed locally and never transmitted externally.

7. Data Deletion

All app data can be deleted at any time from within the app:

Settings → Data Management → Delete All Data

Uninstalling the app will also permanently remove all stored data from your device.

8. Data Deletion Request

To request deletion of any data associated with your use of the app, please contact us at: smartnurse.myroster@gmail.com

9. Children's Privacy

My Roster is intended for healthcare professionals. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
smartnurse.myroster@gmail.com
