📋 Privacy Policy

My Roster — by Smart Nurse

Last updated: July 2026

Summary: My Roster stores all your data locally on your device only. We do not collect or transmit personal data, except through Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

1. Introduction

My Roster ("the App") is developed by Smart Nurse. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application.

2. Data We Collect

My Roster stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Imported roster files and shift schedules
  • Employee names, titles, departments, and contact numbers (from your imported files)
  • Your selected name from the roster
  • Salary profiles and calculations
  • App settings and preferences
  • Shift guide customizations
  • Notification preferences

None of this data is stored on our servers or shared with third parties.

3. Contact Numbers

If your imported roster file contains employee contact numbers, these numbers are:

  • Stored locally on your device only
  • Never uploaded or transmitted to any server
  • Used only to enable direct calling, SMS, and WhatsApp features within the app
  • Never shared with any third party

4. Camera and Photo Library Access

The app may request access to your camera or photo library solely for the purpose of importing roster images for OCR text scanning. These images are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

5. File Access

The app requests file access solely to allow you to import roster files (Excel, PDF, Word, Image, CSV) from your device. These files are processed locally and never transmitted externally.

6. Local Notifications

My Roster may send local notifications to remind you of upcoming shifts, night shifts, weekly summaries, and salary reminders. These notifications are:

  • Scheduled locally on your device
  • Never sent through external servers
  • Fully controlled by you in the app settings
  • Can be disabled at any time

7. Advertising — Google AdMob

My Roster uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to show relevant ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA / GAID)
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data

This data is collected by Google AdMob according to their own privacy policy. You can review Google's privacy policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings.

8. Third-Party Services

Other than Google AdMob, My Roster does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or data collection services.

9. Data Deletion

All app data can be deleted at any time:

Settings → Data Management → Delete All Data

Uninstalling the app will also permanently remove all locally stored data from your device.

10. Data Deletion Request

To request deletion of any data, please contact us at: smart7nurse@gmail.com

Or visit our Data Deletion Request page.

11. Children's Privacy

My Roster is intended for professional use. It is not directed at children under the age of 13.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
