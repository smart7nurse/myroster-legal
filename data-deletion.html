🗑️ Data Deletion Request

My Roster — by Smart Nurse

Last updated: July 2026

Important: My Roster stores all roster data, employee information, contact numbers, salary profiles, and settings locally on your device only. No personal data is stored on our servers. You can delete all your data instantly from within the app.

What Data Does My Roster Store?

My Roster stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Imported roster files and shift schedules
  • Employee names, titles, departments, and contact numbers
  • Your selected name from the roster
  • Salary profiles and calculations
  • App settings and preferences
  • Shift guide customizations
  • Notification preferences

None of this data is stored on our servers or shared with third parties.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1 — Delete from within the App (Recommended)

This is the fastest way to delete all your data:

  1. Open My Roster on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings ⚙️ icon in the top bar.
  3. Go to Data Management.
  4. Tap Delete All Data.
  5. Confirm the deletion.

This will permanently delete all rosters, employee data, contact numbers, salary profiles, settings, and personal information stored in the app.

Option 2 — Delete Specific Data

You can also delete specific types of data:

  1. Open Settings ⚙️
  2. Go to Data Management
  3. Choose one of:
    • Delete Imported Rosters — removes all roster files and employee data
    • Delete Salary Records — removes all salary profiles
    • Clear Saved Name — removes your selected name

Option 3 — Uninstall the App

Uninstalling My Roster from your device will automatically remove all locally stored data associated with the app.

Option 4 — Submit a Deletion Request

If you need assistance or have concerns about your data, you can submit a deletion request by contacting us directly:

Contact Smart Nurse

📧 Email: smart7nurse@gmail.com

Please include in your email:
  • Subject: Data Deletion Request — My Roster
  • Your name (optional)
  • Description of data you want deleted
  • Device type (Android / iOS)

We will respond within 7 business days.
